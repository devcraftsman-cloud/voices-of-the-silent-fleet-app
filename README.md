# Voices of the Silent Fleet

**2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars**  
**Team:** The Human Architech (Chicago, IL Local Event)

## Overview
**Voices of the Silent Fleet** is an interactive, story-driven educational web application designed for school-age space enthusiasts (ages 10–18) and curious learners. Instead of treating historic spacecraft as static museum entries, the app lets learners explore NASA hardware across the Moon, Mars, and interstellar space through 3-chapter mission timelines, scale telemetry comparisons, and first-person conversations powered by Gemini.

## Featured Spacecraft
- **Apollo 11 Lunar Laser Ranging Retroreflector (LRRR)** — *The Moon (Sea of Tranquility, 1969–Present)*
- **Opportunity Rover ("Oppy")** — *Mars (Meridiani Planum, 2004–2019)*
- **InSight Mars Lander** — *Mars (Elysium Planitia, 2018–2022)*
- **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter ("Ginny")** — *Mars (Jezero Crater, 2021–2024)*
- **Voyager 1 Space Probe** — *Interstellar Space (1977–Present)*

## NASA Data Sources & Archives
All mission timelines, coordinates, NSSDC IDs, and scientific findings are grounded in official NASA open archives:
- NASA Science Mission Directorate (SMD)
- NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDC) Master Catalog
- NASA Planetary Data System (PDS)
- NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Mission Archives

## AI Transparency & Tech Stack
- **Frontend & Backend:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Node.js/Express
- **AI Storytelling:** Google Gemini (`gemini-2.5-flash`) with spacecraft-specific system instructions grounded in NASA mission records.
